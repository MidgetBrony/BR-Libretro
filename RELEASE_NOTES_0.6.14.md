# BR-Libretro 0.6.14

- Makes procedural GB, GBC, and GBA retail boxes visibly three-dimensional.
- Adds a thicker, darkened box body and side walls that remain distinct from furniture surfaces.
- Adds a light cardboard front board and visible rim around the supplied platform-frame artwork.
- Raises the artwork slightly above the printed face to prevent it reading as a floor or shelf decal.
